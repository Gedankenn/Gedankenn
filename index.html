About

Computer Engineer (UTFPR) working end to end on embedded products: production Embedded Linux images with Yocto/BitBake, Device Tree (DTS/DTB) and Linux kernel drivers/BSP, and bare-metal Embedded C/C++ on ARM Cortex-M (MSP, STM32, ESP32): bootloaders, OTA, cryptography, MQTT, Ethernet/TCP-IP, CAN, RS-485, I²C and UART.

My background spans electronic security, railway and automotive. My research roots are in motor control: Field-Oriented Control of BLDC/PMSM motors and Extended Kalman Filter state estimation, which I keep exploring in open-source firmware and inverter hardware.

I work in cross-functional teams, listen closely, and treat code review as engineering, not conflict.

Featured projects

Open-source work on GitHub, mostly around power measurement and motor control. Status is stated as it is in each repository.

InstrumentationWorking

power_profiller

Real-time energy profiling for battery-powered electronics. An ESP32 reads a TI INA226 over I²C at ~36 samples/s, streams CSV over serial and serves a live Chart.js dashboard over Wi-Fi with no cloud. A Python analyzer adds burst detection and CR2032 battery-life estimates.

Motor control firmwareWork in progress

ESP32-FOC

ESP32 firmware toward Field-Oriented Control of a BLDC motor. Today it runs open-loop V/F control: a potentiometer on the ADC sets the speed reference, a FreeRTOS task integrates the electrical angle, and MCPWM drives three half-bridges at 1 kHz.

CESP-IDFMCPWMBLDC

Power electronicsStudy

3-phase-inverter

Six-switch inverter for BLDC motors: three IR2110 gate drivers and six IRF540N MOSFETs (VDD 5 V, VCC 15 V), documented with PCB renders and datasheet notes. It is the power stage for ESP32-FOC.

IR2110IRF540NPCBGPL-3.0

Traction inverter · PMSMPhase 0 · modeling

OTPC · Open Traction PMSM Controller

An open-source FOC platform aimed at electric-motorcycle traction drives, built simulation-first. The repository has the PMSM model with Clarke/Park transforms and dq dynamics in Python, plus a written theory doc. STM32 firmware and the power stage are planned.

BLDC simulation with FOC + Extended Kalman Filter

Simulation of a brushless DC motor under Field-Oriented Control, with an Extended Kalman Filter estimating rotor speed. The repository holds the full derivation, the thesis (in Portuguese) and the result figures.
